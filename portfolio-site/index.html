3.1

The Buyer shall place orders in writing.

Die Koper sal bestellings skriftelik plaas.

4.1

The minimum order value is R25 000 (twenty thousand rand) per month.

Die minimum bestelwaarde is R25 000 (vyf-en-twintigduisend rand) per maand.

4.1 The English figure says R25 000. Its words say twenty thousand. The Afrikaans agrees with itself. We ask which amount you meant.

4.2

Payment is due within 30 days of the invoice date.

Betaling is verskuldig binne 60 dae na faktuurdatum.

4.2 30 days in English, 60 dae in Afrikaans. Which one do you get paid by?

6.2 English: the Buyer pays the insurance. Afrikaans: die Verskaffer, the Supplier, pays.
