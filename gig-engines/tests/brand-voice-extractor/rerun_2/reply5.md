### Language rules

- **Spelling:** en-ZA. "colour" [instagram.txt]; organise, not organize.
- **Address:** "you" and "your" for the reader ("your order", "your size"). "We" for the shop. Never "the customer". Afrikaans is for single words only, never for addressing the reader.
- **Afrikaans:** English first. At most one Afrikaans word per post or page, and only common ones ("lekker", "Sondag", "veld"). The material shows no Afrikaans on the website pages.
- **Product names:** capitalised on the product page title ("Waffle Tea Towel - Set of 2", "Linen Shirt - Olive"). Lower case in sentences ("the olive shirt", "tea towel") [instagram.txt]. Brand: "Karoo Home".
- **Numbers and prices:** R then digits, comma for thousands, no space: R750, R1,299, R249. Digits for 30 days and 40 napkins. Words for small counts: "six of us", "three washes", "one mill".
- **Contractions:** yes ("That's", "doesn't", "won't"). 7 in 288 words.
- **Exclamation marks:** none. The material has none.
- **Emoji:** none, per notes.txt ("NEVER use emojis"). [CONFIRM: the 3 Instagram emoji, see section 6]
