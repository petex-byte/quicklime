## 4. Before and After

### Example 1. Channel: website home page

**Before** (agency copy, the client's own weak line) [home.txt]:
"Elevate your living space with our curated collection of premium homeware. Our premium products are crafted with the highest quality materials for the discerning customer."

**After:**
"Made slowly, in small runs, in Prince Albert. We buy our linen from one mill and we know the people who run it. Linen that gets softer every time you wash it. That's the whole trick."

**What changed:**
- Banned words removed: elevate, curated, premium, crafted, highest quality, discerning, living space (section 2, Words to Ban).
- Trait 1 (plain-spoken, not posh): "collection" and "materials" became real things: a town, a mill, linen.
- Tone (Casual 2, Enthusiastic 4): two long sentences of 11 and 14 words became short ones that end on a shrug.

Every line in the After is already in their own material (home.txt and about.txt), which shows the voice is there to use.

### Example 2. Channel: product page (linen shirt)

**Before** (copy the client hates, competitor) [hated-competitor.txt]:
"Indulge in the ultimate luxury experience. Our exquisite, artisanal linens are meticulously curated to elevate your sanctuary and unlock a world of effortless elegance."

**After:**
"100% linen, cut loose, so it moves when you do. It wrinkles. Linen likes it that way. Wash cold, hang dry, skip the iron."

**What changed:**
- Banned words removed: indulge, ultimate, luxury, exquisite, artisanal, meticulously, curated, elevate, sanctuary, unlock, effortless elegance (section 2).
- Trait 1 (plain-spoken, not posh): vague claims became facts: the cloth, the cut, how to wash it.
- Trait 3 (cheeky, not clownish) and Tone: the flaw is owned with a dry line ("It wrinkles. Linen likes it that way."). Two sentences (6 and 18 words) became four short ones.

The After uses the shirt page's own facts (product-linen-shirt.txt). No price, size or colour claims were added.
