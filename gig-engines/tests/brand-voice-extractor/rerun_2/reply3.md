That is 3 small lines in 288 words, with no jokes that need a set-up and no exclamation marks. It is cheeky. I cannot call it "super funny" from this material.
4. **"like a friend who lives on a farm" [notes.txt] vs the material.** The word "farm" appears nowhere in the pages or captions. The nearest are place and land words: "Prince Albert" and "the veld".
5. **Who the shop talks to.** The shop writes about itself ("we/our/us" 12 times) twice as often as it talks to the reader ("you/your" 6 times). A friend asks about you. This is a habit to watch, not a firm clash.
6. **The award.** Annelie writes "I think we won some kind of award at a design fair in 2022?? not sure, check" [notes.txt]. Nothing in the pages mentions it. It stays out of the guideline until she confirms.

## 3. Three proposed traits

**Trait 1: We are plain-spoken, not posh.**
- "We don't do seasons." [about.txt]
- "We buy our linen from one mill and we know the people who run it." [about.txt]
- "That's the whole trick." [home.txt]
- Meaning: short words, real things, no fancy nouns.

**Trait 2: We are warm like a neighbour, not like a brand.**
- "A real person answers. Usually Annelie." [about.txt]
- "Come say hi. Bring your own bag." [instagram.txt]
- Meaning: we name people, we tell you what to do, and we sound pleased to see you.

**Trait 3: We are cheeky, not clownish.**
- "Wear it to the market, wear it to a wedding, wear it to bed. We won't tell." [product-linen-shirt.txt]
- "Behind the scenes: Thabo cutting 40 napkins before lunch. Show-off." [instagram.txt]
- Also: "Wash it hot, wash it cold. It doesn't care." [product-tea-towel.txt]
- Meaning: one dry wink per piece, at the end, never a pun or a shout.
- I folded "funny" into this trait because the proof is a few dry lines, not "super funny".
