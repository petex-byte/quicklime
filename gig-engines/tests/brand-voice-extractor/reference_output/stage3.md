## 3. Proposed voice traits (for your approval)

**Trait 1. We are warm and personal, not corporate.**
- Meaning: a real person is behind every line, and we name them.
- Proof: "phone us. A real person answers. Usually Annelie." [about.txt]
- Proof: "Behind the scenes: Thabo cutting 40 napkins before lunch. Show-off." [instagram.txt]
- Extra: "we know the people who run it" [about.txt]
- Do / don't: Do: "Thabo cut 40 napkins before lunch." Don't: "Our dedicated team of artisans." (my example, not from the material)

**Trait 2. We are plain-spoken, not posh.**
- Meaning: everyday words, short sentences, no fancy adjectives.
- Proof: "Wash cold, hang dry, skip the iron." [product-linen-shirt.txt]
- Proof: "Linen that gets softer every time you wash it. That's the whole trick." [home.txt]
- Extra: "Free delivery over R750. Returns within 30 days, no fuss." [home.txt]
- Do / don't: Do: "Made slowly, in small runs, in Prince Albert." Don't: "Our premium products are crafted with the highest quality materials for the discerning customer." [home.txt, agency copy]

**Trait 3. We are cheeky and sure of ourselves, with a dry end-line, not loud or gag-heavy.**
- Meaning: we say a thing straight, then add a short tease. No exclamation marks, no puns.
- Proof: "Wear it to the market, wear it to a wedding, wear it to bed. We won't tell." [product-linen-shirt.txt]
- Proof: "Wash it hot, wash it cold. It doesn't care." [product-tea-towel.txt]
- Extra: "We don't do seasons." [about.txt]
- Do / don't: Do: "It wrinkles. Linen likes it that way." Don't: "Say hello to the softest, most amazing linen ever!!"

**ASPIRATION (client wants it; no real proof yet): "We are funny, not just charming."**
- Closest line in her material: "Show-off." [instagram.txt]
- Why it is not a trait yet: one line and a two-word jab. Her notes say captions "crack them up". We have 5 captions and no jokes beyond a tease.
- Suggestion: fold "funny" into Trait 3 as the dry end-line, and tell her in section 6 we need more captions to prove a stronger level. Say if you want it as its own trait instead (it would then carry the ASPIRATION label with one quote).

Also noted, as a rule rather than a trait: **no emojis** (her words). It goes in Quick Rules and Words to Ban, and the Instagram clash goes in section 6.
