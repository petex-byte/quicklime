## Observations for later (not yet analysis)

- The strongest evidence for "cheeky" is the short closer line. It is much stronger on the product pages and about page than on the home page or Instagram.
- "Warm" shows up through named people (Annelie, Thabo), small true details (the hot garage, the kettle) and "phone us. A real person answers."
- "Super funny" (notes.txt) is not borne out by the captions we have: they are charming and dry, but there are no jokes beyond a one-word tease. Five captions is a thin sample, so I will flag this as unproven, not wrong.
- No example of "opinions" beyond a few lines ("We don't do seasons." "It wrinkles. Linen likes it that way."). That is enough for one trait, but only just.
- We have no sale email, no customer-service message (happy or complaint) and no delay or out-of-stock message. The Stage 6 channel table will need to be marked as built from the voice, not proven from examples, in section 6 as agreed.
