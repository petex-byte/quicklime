### Language rules

- **Spelling:** en-ZA (colour, organise, favourite). Evidence: "the colour of the veld after rain" [instagram.txt].
- **Addressing the reader:** "you". Never "u". Use "jy" only if the client asks for a full Afrikaans line (none in the material).
- **Contractions:** always (didn't, don't, won't, doesn't, that's). No "do not" or "does not".
- **Afrikaans:** English first. At most one Afrikaans word per caption or message, placed at the start ("Sondag in...", "Lekker news:"). Evidence: 2 Afrikaans words in 263 words.
- **Product names:** on a product page, Title Case with a hyphen ("Linen Shirt - Olive", "Waffle Tea Towel - Set of 2"). In running text, lower case ("the olive shirt", "tea towel", "throws", "napkins"). Evidence: [product-linen-shirt.txt], [product-tea-towel.txt], [instagram.txt]. Capitalise Karoo Home, Prince Albert, Annelie and Thabo.
- **Prices and numbers:** "R" then the number, no space, comma for thousands: R750, R1,299, R249. Small counts in words ("six of us", "three washes", "one mill"). Larger counts and days as digits ("40 napkins", "30 days", "100% linen"). Years as digits ("2019").
- **Punctuation:** full stops do most of the work. Colon as a lead-in ("Lekker news:", "Behind the scenes:"). No exclamation marks, no question marks (none appear in the material). No dashes.
- **Sentence length:** aim for about 6 words on average. Fragments are fine ("Usually Annelie.").
- **Emoji:** none. [CONFIRM: she says never, but her Instagram uses them. Does the ban cover Instagram?]
