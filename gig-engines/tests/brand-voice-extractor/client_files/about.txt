OUR STORY
Annelie started Karoo Home in 2019 with one sewing machine and a garage that got very hot in January.
She wanted tea towels that didn't fall apart after three washes. The shops didn't have them, so she made them.
Today there are six of us. We still cut every piece by hand.
We buy our linen from one mill and we know the people who run it.
We don't do seasons. We make things until they run out, then we make them again.
If something goes wrong with your order, phone us. A real person answers. Usually Annelie.
