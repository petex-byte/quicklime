SUPPLY AGREEMENT

between

FYNBOS TEXTILES (PTY) LTD (Registration number 2019/123456/07) ("the Supplier")

and

KAROO HOME (PTY) LTD (Registration number 2021/654321/07) ("the Buyer")

1. DEFINITIONS
1.1 "Agreement" means this supply agreement and Annexure A.
1.2 "Goods" means the linen products listed in Annexure A.
1.3 "Business Day" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in South Africa.

2. TERM
2.1 This Agreement starts on 1 November 2026 and runs for 24 months.

3. ORDERS
3.1 The Buyer shall place orders in writing.
3.2 The Supplier shall confirm each order within 2 Business Days.

4. PRICE AND PAYMENT
4.1 The minimum order value is R25 000 (twenty thousand rand) per month.
4.2 Payment is due within 30 days of the invoice date.
4.3 Late payments carry interest at 2% per month.

5. DELIVERY
5.1 The Supplier shall deliver the Goods within 5 Business Days of order confirmation.
5.2 The Buyer shall recieve the Goods at its warehouse in Paarl.

6. RISK
6.1 Risk in the Goods passes to the Buyer on delivery.
6.2 The Buyer shall pay for transport insurance.

7. DEFECTS
7.1 The Supplier shall replace defective Goods within 10 Business Days of written notice.
7.2 Claims for defects must be made within 14 days of delivery.

8. INSURANCE
8.1 The Supplier shall keep public liability insurance.
8.2 The Supplier shall give the Buyer a copy of the policy on request.
8.3 The insurance cover shall not be less than R1 000 000 per claim.

9. TERMINATION
9.1 Either party may end this Agreement on 3 months' written notice.
9.1 A party may end this Agreement immediately if the other party breaches clause 4 and does not fix the breach within 7 days of written notice.

10. ASSIGNMENT
10.1 The Buyer may not assign this Agreement without the Supplier's written consent.

11. DISPUTES
11.1 The parties shall first try to settle any dispute by negotiation.
11.2 Subject to clause 11.3, any dispute not settled within 30 days goes to arbitration in Cape Town.
