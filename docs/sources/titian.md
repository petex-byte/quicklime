## Kept out of the copy

- **Venus's face and hands finished after Titian's death (1576)**: getdailyart only. Weak.
- **Instrument in Venus's hand (recorder/flute)**: visible but not named in any source found. Copy says only "stopped playing".
- **Sheet music on the ledge, sword on the floor**: visible, not discussed in sources found.
- **Fitzwilliam version's provenance (Rudolf II, Swedish plunder, Queen Christina)**: true of the other painting, not this one.
- **Earlier Roman provenance** (PubHist marks it with a question mark).
