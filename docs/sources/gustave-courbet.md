## Dropped or kept out

- **"The French state briefly considered buying it."** The Met says so; no independent second source found (Wikipedia appears to repeat the Met). It would have been the twist. Kept out. **weak**
- **Sold to the stockbroker Lepel-Cointet, May 1866.** Only a Sotheby's catalogue note (citing Chu's *Letters*). Kept out. **weak**
- **The model was Joanna Hiffernan.** Wikipedia says "likely"; sources hedge. Kept out. **weak**
- **Cézanne carried a photo of it in his wallet.** The Met says "apparently"; Daily Art says "was said to". A reported anecdote, not a documented fact. Kept out. **weak**
- **Nieuwerkerke's role.** No source found linking him to the 1864 rejection or 1866 acceptance. Not used.
