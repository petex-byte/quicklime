## Disputed / kept out of copy

- Pareja's birth year: Met gives ca. 1608–1670 (older Met title 1606–1670); Wikipedia variants give c. 1606 and c. 1610. Not used.
- Palomino's story that Pareja painted in secret and Philip IV had him freed is treated by modern scholarship as a narrative without 17th-century documentary basis (Hispanopedia summary of the Met 2023 research). **Not used.** The copy relies on the 1650 manumission document instead.
- Who spoke the "truth" line (painters in general vs. Andreas Schmidt): attribution in copy is kept general.
- "$5.5 million" dollar figure: not found in any source. Not used.
- Met accession year (sale 1970, accession number 1971.86): copy avoids stating when it entered the collection.
