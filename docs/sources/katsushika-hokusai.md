## Weak or dropped

- **Print run and survivors:** estimates run 5,000–8,000 printed, and 100 to "several hundred" surviving (Art Fund/British Museum; MFA Boston cites a 2022 census of 113). Sources disagree, so this is kept out of the copy.
- **Who chose the La Mer cover:** the Met says Debussy curated his covers; the Sibley entry credits the publisher. Kept out.
- **Signature meaning:** the signature reads 北斎改為一筆, *Hokusai aratame Iitsu hitsu*. Commonly glossed "from the brush of Iitsu, formerly Hokusai", but no specialist source confirmed the gloss this pass. Not used.
