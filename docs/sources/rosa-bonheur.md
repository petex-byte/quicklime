## Dropped or kept out

- **The permit date as a twist.** One blog (Paris Zigzag) and one café display report a permit dated 12 May 1857, which would postdate most of the painting. Other sources say "by 1850" or "in the 1850s", and the van Slyke article is plural ("permits"). Sources disagree, so the copy gives no date. **disputed**
- **Queen Victoria.** The painting was shown privately to the Queen in 1855. The Royal Collection says Windsor; the Met essay and Journal of Antiques say Buckingham Palace. Location disputed, so it's kept out of the copy. **disputed**
- **"The garments of my sex were a constant bother."** Widely quoted but the wording varies ("clothing" vs "garments") and no source excerpt tied it to a page. Not used. **weak**
- **The rider's hat and gaze.** The audio guide mentions a hat; the Met photo at this size doesn't show it clearly, and no source says the figure looks out. Not used.
- **Signature.** Met says it's dated 1853, but the signature isn't legible in the 800px photo, so no beat is built on it.
