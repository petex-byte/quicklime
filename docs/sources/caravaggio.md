## Kept out of the copy

- **Riina "used it as a rug":** one secondary excerpt only, contradicted by others. **Weak**, dropped.
- **Mafia as the thieves:** widely accepted, not proven. The copy says only that informants told conflicting stories. Some authorities think amateurs stole it after an August 1969 RAI programme mentioned its value (Artnet; Wikipedia).
- **$20 million FBI valuation, FBI Top Ten Art Crimes list:** Artnet and Wikipedia. Not needed; re-check before using.
- **Switzerland:** Grado (2018) claimed a sale to a Swiss dealer; Cuppone's book discusses it. Unproven.
- **Youth in the foreground, back to the viewer, turning to Joseph:** one excerpt. Not used.
