## Disputed / kept out of copy

- Which Trudaine commissioned it: Met says Charles-Louis Trudaine de Montigny; Wikipedia and EBSCO say Charles-Michel Trudaine de la Sablière (10,000 livres per EBSCO). Copy avoids naming him.
- Exact execution date: the Lyon academy dictionary says the Trudaine sons were guillotined with their friend André Chénier on 7 Thermidor Year II (25 July 1794), two days before Robespierre's fall. **weak**: one source only, and another search summary suggested a different month. Kept out; the heading is "1794" only.
- Number of people present: Phaedo names about fifteen; not counted against the painting. Not used.
- Reynolds calling it "in every sense perfect": one secondary source only. Not used.
