## Dropped or kept out

- **Who sold it in 1923.** Sources disagree (Durand-Ruel vs. Durand-Ruel's son). Kept out.
- **"About $2 million today."** A rough conversion from a non-authoritative source. Kept out.
- **The "impudence" letter.** Smithsonian says Renoir complained of a sitter's "impudence"; no second source. Kept out. **weak**
- **Renoir broke his right arm in 1880 and painted left-handed.** NPR only (citing Barbara Ehrlich White). Kept out. **weak**
- **Aline's job** (waitress / dressmaker / seamstress). Sources disagree. Kept out.
