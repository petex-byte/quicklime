## Left out

- Nippon Television funding the restoration in exchange for photo rights: confirmed in excerpts, but trivia next to the cleaning itself.
- The goitre: the 2016 Lazzeri study argues for one; the same authors note later writers over-read the sonnet. Disputed, not in copy.
- Bramante's hanging scaffold and its holes in the vault: from Condivi/Vasari tradition, found only in secondary course pages this pass. **weak**, not in copy.
- Exact unveiling date (31 Oct vs 1 Nov 1512): not settled in the sources found.
