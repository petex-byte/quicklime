## Weak or dropped

- **Joseph Bonaparte's baggage at Vitoria:** the NG catalogue says "may have"; Hay claimed a Brussels purchase. Kept out except as "still unclear".
- **Margaret of Austria's 1516 inventory** (and a note about a lock): NG transcription only this pass. Kept out.
- **Marriage, betrothal or memorial readings, and "she isn't pregnant":** interpretations. Kept out.
