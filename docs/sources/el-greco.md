## Kept out of the copy

- **Return to Toledo in 2014** for the 400th anniversary: not confirmed. The Met's 2014 "El Greco in New York" show included it.
- **Conde de los Arcos collection, 1630s**: Met catalogue says it "may be" this picture. Left out.
- **Cassatt's role in the Havemeyers' El Greco buying**: Met Bulletin 1981 describes it in general; not tied to this painting in what I found.
