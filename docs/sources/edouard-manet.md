## Dropped or kept out

- **Huysmans on the "too blue" water.** A widely repeated story that Huysmans defended the blue water against critics. No excerpt confirmed the wording. Kept out. **weak**
- **Hung high at the Salon.** The Met says it "may have been hung high". Hedged and single-source. Kept out. **weak**
- **The year the Havemeyers bought it.** The Met excerpt gives Cassatt's 1895 recommendation, not the purchase date, so the copy says she told them to buy it, not when they did.
