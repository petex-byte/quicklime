## Left out

- The c. 1785 copy in the Nuremberg museums, attributed with a question mark to Küfner, and the idea that he swapped the original for it. Treated as open by the museum itself; X-ray and infrared studies haven't settled it. Not in copy.
- City council minutes not mentioning the sale, and Nuremberg's 1822 request to get the panel back: Nuremberg museums only — **weak**, kept out of copy until a second source turns up.
- Hair "actually reddish-blond": single secondary source. Not in copy.
- Templar/Masonic reading of the hand: single commentary. Not in copy.
