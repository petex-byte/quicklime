## Left out

- Simonetta Vespucci as the model: popular, not checked this pass. Not in copy.
- Botticelli burning his own work in Savonarola's bonfires: not checked. Not in copy.
- First documented by Vasari, mid-16th century, at the Medici villa of Castello; commissioned by Lorenzo di Pierfrancesco de' Medici: Google Arts & Culture and DailyArt (not documented until 1550). Good material but crowded out; usable in a later revision.
