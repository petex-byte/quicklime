## Disputed / kept out of copy

- Dating: the Met dates it 1787–88; the Italian Met page reportedly suggests it may have been painted after the boy's death (1792). Not used. Copy keeps the Met's 1787–88.
- Provenance: Fundación Goya says the Altamira family sold it in 1878; owned by Bernstein (Paris) 1903–25, then Duveen sold it to Jules Bache in 1927; Bache's estate held it until 1949. Only one source per step this pass, so the earlier draft line about the 1878 sale was dropped.
- Which side the cage and cats are on: sources disagree in wording; the image shows cats left, cage right (viewer's view). Copy avoids left/right.
