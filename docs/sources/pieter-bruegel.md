## Kept out of the copy

- **Cock-throwing game** (Bringley) and **bathers in the pond** (Bringley says nude monks): the figures are too small to identify on the 800px image; one source only.
- **Cels sold it cheap to join his fiancée in Australia**: one secondary source (Andrew Graham-Dixon archive excerpt). Weak.
- **"August–September"** as the season of this panel: one source says "probably". Left out.
- **The 1566 pledge was to the city of Antwerp**: the documented writ was for De Bruyne's debt; the city's later acquisition (and 1594/5 gift to Archduke Ernst) is reported separately. Not used.
- **Dimensions**: Met gives 119 × 162 cm overall; one excerpt quoted 116.5 × 159.5 cm painted surface. Ratio taken from the Met image file.
