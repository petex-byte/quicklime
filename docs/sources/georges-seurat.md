## Dropped or kept out

- **The price Bartlett paid.** One source says $22,000; the $20,000 figure often repeated wasn't found. Kept out.
- **The colour change "first noted in 1892".** Only an AIC abstract. Kept out in favour of "within a few years". **weak**
- **"They see poetry in what I have done. No, I apply my method."** Secondhand, reported by Charles Angrand to H.-E. Cross in 1891, with several English versions. Not used as the quote. **weak**
- **Monet and Renoir stayed out of the 1886 Impressionist show because of Seurat.** One source says both "found excuses"; not enough to state as fact. Kept out. **weak**
- **The woman fishing as a pun on *pêcher / pécher*.** Not checked this pass. Kept out.
- **AIC never lends the painting.** Not checked. Kept out.

## Dimensions

AIC (via Google Arts & Culture): 207.6 × 308 cm (81.7 × 121.25 in). One third-party page gives 207.5 × 308.1 cm. The story uses 1600 × 1078.
