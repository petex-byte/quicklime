## Dropped or kept out

- **"The woman may not be the child's mother."** One art-history site (Sartle) says the woman is not necessarily the parent; the AIC calls her a caregiver. Only one explicit source, so it stayed out of the copy. **weak**
- **Basin and pitcher.** Commonly described, but no source excerpt confirmed them this pass, so no beat names them.
- **"A third of her paintings are mother and child."** Only New World Encyclopedia. Kept out. **weak**
- **Whether this was the AIC's first Cassatt.** Not established. Kept out.

## Dimensions

Sources disagree slightly. AIC API record: 101.3 × 67.3 cm (39 15/16 × 26 1/2 in). Google Arts & Culture / AMICO: 100.3 × 66.1 cm (39 1/2 × 26 in). The story uses the AIC API ratio: 1063 × 1600.
