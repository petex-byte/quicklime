## Dropped or kept out

- **"He painted it in a month."** Two search excerpts say so, both reporting Gauguin's own account, and both from non-museum sites. Kept out. **weak**
- **The white bird holding a lizard ("the futility of words").** Gauguin's letter describes it, but only one source (Fortean Times) quoted the passage this pass, and the search budget ran out before a second. Kept out. **weak**. A strong candidate beat once confirmed
- **The central figure picking fruit.** Commonly described, not confirmed this pass. Kept out.
- **The exact date of Aline's death and her age.** Sources conflict. Kept out.

## Dimensions

MFA: 139.1 × 374.6 cm (54 3/4 × 147 1/2 in). The story uses 1600 × 594. A very wide canvas: check that the zooms read on a phone.
