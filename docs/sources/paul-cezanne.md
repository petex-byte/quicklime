# Paul Cézanne — The Card Players (1890s), Musée d'Orsay

Internal sources list for `src/content/stories/paul-cezanne.ts`. Each line of copy maps to a row below.

Featured version: *Les Joueurs de cartes*, oil on canvas, 47.5 x 57 cm, Musée d'Orsay, Paris (RF 1969), the two-player version. Bequest of Isaac de Camondo, 1911 (Wikidata; the Orsay snippet seen didn't show the credit line), at the Jeu de Paume 1947–86, at the Orsay since 1986.
