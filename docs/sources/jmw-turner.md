# J. M. W. Turner: The Fighting Temeraire (1839)

Internal sources list for `src/content/stories/jmw-turner.ts`. Each line of copy maps to a row below.

**Fact-check pass (16 Oct 2026):** every row was checked against the source page itself and the supporting sentence is quoted in the table.

**Why this work, not Rain, Steam and Speed:** the Temeraire hook (masts painted back on) is confirmed by the National Gallery's own notes and by Royal Museums Greenwich, and the close (bequest, 2005 poll, £20 note) has primary sources. Rain, Steam and Speed's best-known anecdote (Turner putting his head out of a train window in a storm) was not cross-checked this pass and is usually traced to one later recollection.

National Gallery NG524, oil on canvas, 90.7 x 121.6 cm. Image aspect 1600 x 1193.

## Weak or dropped

- **Two tugs, not one** (the *London* and the *Samson*): Hero Magazine only this pass. Kept out.
- **Wrong hull colours** (white and gold vs. yellow and black): National Gallery notes only. Kept out.
- **"Sail to steam" reading:** standard interpretation, not stated as fact in the copy ("Steam, pulling sail" is literal).
