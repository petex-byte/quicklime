## Kept out of the copy

- Bell "usually worked in watercolour" and made "perhaps half-a-dozen oils": one source only (Strauss & Co catalogue note). **weak**.
- The idea that the 1952 Granger Bay pageant was staged after Bell's painting: no source found. Kept out.
