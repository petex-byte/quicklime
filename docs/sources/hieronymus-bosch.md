## Weak or dropped

- **Playability of the "butt music":** contested (Early Music Muse). Kept out.
- **Which patron commissioned it** (Engelbert II vs. Henry III of Nassau): depends on the disputed date. Kept out.
- **Grey wings as "before the sun and moon" or as the Flood:** competing readings. Kept out.
