# Node 1: Brand Strategy (Four-Point Framework)

> Rule for every line in this folder: pineapple words only. If you can't see it, touch it or picture it, cut it.

## 1. Target: who has the money and the headache?

The owner of a small online shop.

- 2 to 20 staff. Sells $100k to $3m a year (R2m to R50m).
- Runs a Shopify or WooCommerce store. Sends 4 to 8 sale emails a month.
- Signs supplier deals, lease papers, influencer contracts and shop terms.
- South Africa first (English and Afrikaans buyers and suppliers). Then UK, US and Australia.
- Signs off designs at 11 pm, on a phone, in bed.

They have a card on file and a launch date on the wall. They do not have a team for the dull, risky parts.

## 2. Problem: what is the actual mess?

They hire three strangers for three jobs: one for the site, one for the emails, one to "look over" the contract. Each one sends a file and goes quiet.

The owner becomes the glue. They copy text from a Google Doc into Figma. They paste 400 products into Shopify by hand. They email the contract PDF to a cousin who "did a bit of law."

Things break in the gaps:

- The Black Friday email goes out with a dead button.
- The supplier contract says **30 days** in English and **60 dae** in Afrikaans.
- 400 products go live with the old price.
- The checkout screen looks great on a laptop and cuts off the "Pay" button on an iPhone SE.

## 3. Insight: the hidden truth

**Owners don't lose money on ugly work. They lose it on small mistakes nobody saw.**

A plain email still sells. A wrong date in a contract, a broken link or a wrong price costs cash, in public, with their name on it. They don't want more talent. They want a second pair of eyes that reads every line before it goes out.

## 4. Strategy: our sharp angle

**Sell the red pen, not the pixel.**

Every order ships with a "caught it" sheet: a marked-up PDF that lists each mistake we found and fixed, with a page number or screenshot. The buyer sees the work *and* the catch.

Why only we can do this:

| Petri | Chané | Claude (checked by us) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Figma screens, click-through mock-ups, email design, hand-coded HTML email | 10 years in law. Reads contracts line by line in English and Afrikaans | Cleans messy product sheets into store files. Writes prompt packs. Runs the first pass fast |
| Catches the broken button | Catches the wrong date | Catches the wrong price |

**Line we own:** *We catch what slips.*

One inbox. One deadline. Every file read twice (once by the maker, once by the other partner) before it reaches you.

### Proof devices (use them everywhere)

1. **The red pen.** Logo mark, gallery frames, the "caught it" sheet.
2. **The number.** "We found 14 mistakes in your file." Count every catch. Put the count in the delivery message.
3. **Two faces.** Real photo of Petri and Chané. Buyers on Fiverr buy people.
