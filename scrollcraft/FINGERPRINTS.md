# Fingerprints

Registry for scroll-craft builds in this repo. A new build must differ from every row on at least 4 of 6 dimensions. Rows are append-only.

| Build | Grammar | Nav treatment | Hero device | Act-sequence shape | Close pattern | Signature move | World | Port |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| red-pen-pixel | Chaptered editorial | Fixed margin folio (page number + chapter title), no bar | Title page: 4 parallax paper/pen planes, underline drawn from --sc-p | 7 flow chapters, no pins: parallax > in > silence > signature+count > reveal > in > colophon, ~9 vh | Colophon, smallest type, CTA as running text | Catch it yourself: tap words in a bilingual contract, red circles + margin notes, tally vs the check | Paper and ink, code-drawn, no photos | Static HTML on Vercel |

## What is taken

- Chaptered editorial with a margin folio.
- Proofreading-exercise signature (tap-to-catch on real text).
